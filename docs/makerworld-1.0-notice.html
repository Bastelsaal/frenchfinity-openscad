🚀 NEW: Frenchfinity 2.0 is here – fully customizable!

This model is part of Frenchfinity 1.0. Frenchfinity 2.0 brings the whole system into one customizer: pick a part, enter the size of your tool and print a holder that fits it perfectly.

  • 45 parts in one customizer: every 1.0 part, plus a bin, a tray, a welding table plate and more.
  • 100 % compatible: same slot profile, your 1.0 wall anchors and holders keep working.
  • Made for your tool: enter your sizes instead of searching for the right file.
  • Labeled: the parameters are engraved into every part, so reprinting takes seconds.
